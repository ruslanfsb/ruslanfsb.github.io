Wallyn Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Wallyn, a personal budgeting app for iPhone. "We" means the developer of Wallyn.

The same policy is available inside the app: Profile → Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

Your financial data stays on your iPhone. We don't run servers that store it, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell or share your information.

2. Information you enter

Everything you add to Wallyn stays on your device, in the app's private storage: accounts and balances, transactions, bills and subscriptions, goals, debts, budgets, your wishlist, notes, settings, your name, and your answers to the setup questions. We can't see any of it.

Wallyn doesn't connect to your bank and never asks for bank logins.

3. Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a user ID and, the first time only, your name and email address (which may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"). They are saved only on your iPhone and used to show your name in the app. We don't have an account server. Signing out removes them from the app.

4. Subscriptions

Wallyn Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and doesn't share your payment details with us.

We use RevenueCat to confirm whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your App Store purchase and subscription records, and basic device and app information (such as iOS version, app version, and App Store country). RevenueCat never receives your financial data, name, or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

5. Device features

  • Face ID or passcode lock. Handled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives your face data.
  • Notifications. Reminders and summaries are scheduled on your iPhone. No push notification servers are used.
  • Files. Importing a bank statement, exporting transactions, and backing up happen only when you choose to. You decide where the file goes. Files saved to iCloud Drive are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

6. iPhone backups

Like other apps, Wallyn's data may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, according to your Apple settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

7. Analytics and crash reports

Wallyn doesn't include advertising or third-party analytics tools. If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

8. No tracking, no selling

We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, don't use the advertising identifier, and don't sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We don't use your information for targeted advertising.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and change everything in the app at any time.
  • Export your transactions (CSV) or a full backup from Settings.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

Because your financial data never reaches us, there's nothing about it for us to access or delete on our side. For the subscription records kept by RevenueCat, email us and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete them. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information; we will honor those requests and won't treat you differently for making them.

10. Security

Your data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and, when your iPhone has a passcode, by the device's built-in encryption. You can also turn on the Face ID lock and hide amounts on screen. Backup and CSV files you export are not encrypted by Wallyn, so store them somewhere safe.

11. How long data is kept

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete it or the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal obligations.

12. Children

Wallyn is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and in the app and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
