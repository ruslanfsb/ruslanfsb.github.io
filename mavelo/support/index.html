Frequently asked questions

How is "Safe to Spend" calculated?

We take the money in your spending accounts, set aside your bills until payday, your goal savings, and a small safety buffer. What’s left is safe to spend. Tap Why? next to any number in the app to see the full calculation.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Mavelo doesn’t connect to your bank and doesn’t upload your financial data anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, open Settings → Back up to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new one, open Settings → Restore from backup and choose that file. Your data also moves with a normal iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Mavelo → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

I bought Premium, but it isn’t active.

In Mavelo, open Settings → Restore purchases (or tap Restore on the Premium screen). Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

How do I delete all my data?

In Mavelo, open Settings → Delete all data, or simply delete the app.

Can Mavelo connect to my bank?

Not right now, by design: your data stays private on your iPhone. You can type purchases in a second ("coffee 6") or import a CSV statement from your bank.
